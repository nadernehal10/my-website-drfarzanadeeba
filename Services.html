Core Specialty

Infertility Assessment & Treatment

Infertility affects approximately 1 in 6 couples. Dr. Deeba's approach begins with a thorough, systematic evaluation of both partners before developing a personalised treatment pathway that balances clinical evidence with each couple's individual circumstances.

What the Assessment Includes

  • Detailed clinical history and physical examination
  • Hormonal profile: FSH, LH, AMH, TSH, prolactin
  • Transvaginal ultrasound (antral follicle count, uterine assessment)
  • Hysterosalpingography (HSG) for tubal patency
  • Semen analysis and male factor evaluation
  • Genetic screening where indicated

Treatment Options

  • Ovulation induction and timed intercourse
  • Intrauterine Insemination (IUI)
  • In-Vitro Fertilisation (IVF) coordination and support
  • Surgical correction of anatomical factors (laparoscopy, hysteroscopy)

02
Subspecialty

Reproductive Endocrinology

Hormonal disorders are among the most common causes of menstrual irregularity, infertility, and metabolic dysfunction in women. Dr. Deeba holds a fellowship in Reproductive Endocrinology & Infertility (FCPS REI), allowing her to diagnose and manage complex hormonal conditions with precision.

Conditions Treated

  • Polycystic Ovary Syndrome (PCOS)
  • Hypothyroidism and hyperthyroidism
  • Hyperprolactinaemia
  • Premature ovarian insufficiency (POI)
  • Adrenal disorders affecting reproduction
  • Insulin resistance and metabolic syndrome
PCOS cannot be cured, but it can be very effectively managed. With the right combination of lifestyle modification, medication, and — where needed — ovulation induction, most women with PCOS can conceive and manage their long-term metabolic health.
03
General Care

General Gynaecological Care

Routine gynaecological health should not be deferred. Dr. Deeba provides comprehensive general gynaecological consultations for women at every life stage — from adolescence through menopause — with particular attention to early detection and preventive care.

  • Annual pelvic examination and cervical smear (Pap test)
  • Menstrual disorder management (heavy, irregular, painful periods)
  • Uterine fibroids and ovarian cyst evaluation
  • Endometriosis diagnosis and management
  • Contraception counselling and family planning
  • Menopausal transition and HRT guidance
04
Surgical

Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive surgery offers patients faster recovery, smaller incisions, and reduced risk of complications compared to open surgery. Dr. Deeba is experienced in a range of laparoscopic and hysteroscopic procedures for both diagnostic and operative purposes.

Procedures Performed

  • Laparoscopic cystectomy (ovarian cyst removal)
  • Endometriosis resection and ablation
  • Diagnostic laparoscopy for unexplained pain or infertility
  • Myomectomy (fibroid removal)
  • Hysteroscopic polypectomy and septum resection
  • Salpingectomy for ectopic pregnancy
05
Obstetrics

High-Risk Pregnancy

A high-risk pregnancy designation means closer monitoring and a coordinated care plan — not necessarily a complicated outcome. Dr. Deeba works with patients to manage medical, obstetric, and fetal risk factors throughout pregnancy with close follow-up and specialist oversight.

  • Gestational diabetes and pre-existing diabetes
  • Hypertension and pre-eclampsia
  • Multiple gestation (twins, triplets)
  • Prior pregnancy loss or recurrent miscarriage
  • Placenta praevia and placental abnormalities
  • Advanced maternal age (35+)

Reproductive Health Counselling
