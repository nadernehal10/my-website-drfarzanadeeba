About

Dr. Farzana Deeba is a distinguished Gynaecologist and Reproductive Endocrinology & Infertility specialist with over 15 years of clinical and academic experience. She currently serves as Associate Professor in the Department of Gynaecology & Obstetrics at Bangladesh Medical University (BMU), Dhaka.

Her subspecialty training in Reproductive Endocrinology and Infertility, combined with advanced laparoscopic and gynaecological surgical expertise, enables her to provide comprehensive, evidence-based women's health care across all life stages.
