About

Dedicated to Women's Health & Wellbeing

Dr. Farzana Deeba is a distinguished Gynaecologist and Reproductive Endocrinology & Infertility specialist with over 25 years of clinical and academic experience. She currently serves as Associate Professor in the Department of Reproductive Endocrinology & Infertility at Bangladesh Medical University (BMU), Dhaka.

Her academic training in Reproductive Endocrinology and Infertility, combined with her specialisation in laparoscopic and general gynaecological surgery, enables her to provide comprehensive women's health care — from PCOS and menstrual disorders to complex infertility evaluations and high-risk obstetric management.

Services

Specialist Care You Can Trust

Comprehensive women's health services delivered with clinical expertise and personal care.

View all services →

Infertility Assessment & Treatment

Thorough evaluation and individualised treatment plans — including IUI, IVF coordination, hormonal therapy and surgical interventions.

Reproductive Endocrinology

Management of hormonal imbalances, PCOS, thyroid disorders and other endocrine conditions affecting reproductive health.

General Gynaecological Care

Routine check-ups, menstrual disorder management, cervical screening, fibroids, endometriosis and pelvic health.

Laparoscopic Surgery

Minimally invasive surgical procedures for endometriosis, ovarian cysts, fibroids, ectopic pregnancy and diagnostic laparoscopy.

High-Risk Pregnancy

Specialised monitoring and management for pregnancies complicated by hypertension, diabetes, multiple gestation and prior losses.

Reproductive Health Counselling

Compassionate counselling for couples facing infertility, pregnancy loss, family planning, and contraceptive guidance.
