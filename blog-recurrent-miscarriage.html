Recurrent Miscarriage — Causes, Investigations and Treatment in Bangladesh

Experiencing more than one miscarriage is devastating. But recurrent pregnancy loss has identifiable causes in many cases — and effective treatments are available in Dhaka. This guide explains what to expect from a specialist evaluation.

Dr. Farzana Deeba September 2026 7 min read

What is recurrent miscarriage?

Recurrent miscarriage is defined as two or more consecutive pregnancy losses before 20 weeks of gestation. It affects approximately 1–2% of couples trying to conceive. While one miscarriage is common — occurring in up to 20% of confirmed pregnancies — two or more losses warrant thorough investigation to identify any underlying cause.

If you have experienced recurrent miscarriage, please know that in the majority of cases, a cause can be identified and treated — and most couples go on to have a successful pregnancy with appropriate management.

Common causes of recurrent miscarriage

Chromosomal abnormalities

Chromosomal problems in the embryo account for approximately 50–60% of miscarriages. In recurrent cases, either the embryos carry chromosomal abnormalities (often random) or one or both parents carry a chromosomal rearrangement (such as a balanced translocation) that affects pregnancy outcomes.

Uterine abnormalities

  • Uterine septum — a fibrous band dividing the uterine cavity, which can interfere with implantation and blood supply. This is one of the most treatable causes.
  • Fibroids — particularly submucous fibroids that distort the uterine cavity
  • Asherman's syndrome — intrauterine adhesions (scarring) from previous surgery or infection
  • Bicornuate or other shaped uterus

Hormonal and medical causes

  • Antiphospholipid syndrome (APS) — an autoimmune condition that causes blood clots in placental vessels. It is one of the most important and treatable causes of recurrent miscarriage.
  • Thyroid dysfunction — both hypothyroidism and hyperthyroidism increase miscarriage risk
  • Uncontrolled diabetes
  • Luteal phase defect — insufficient progesterone after ovulation to support early pregnancy

Unexplained causes

In approximately 50% of couples with recurrent miscarriage, no specific cause is found even after thorough investigation. This can be difficult to accept, but it does not mean treatment cannot help — many of these couples go on to have successful pregnancies, and supportive care during subsequent pregnancies significantly improves outcomes.

Important: Recurrent miscarriage is not caused by exercise, sex, stress, or most everyday activities. It is not your fault. Specialist evaluation is the right next step.

What investigations are done in Dhaka?

A comprehensive recurrent miscarriage evaluation typically includes:

  • Karyotyping of both partners — blood chromosome analysis to detect balanced translocations
  • Antiphospholipid antibody testing — lupus anticoagulant, anticardiolipin antibodies
  • Thyroid function tests
  • Pelvic ultrasound and/or hysteroscopy — to assess uterine anatomy
  • Thrombophilia screen — in selected cases
  • Products of conception testing — where available, chromosomal analysis of pregnancy tissue can clarify the cause

Treatment options

Antiphospholipid syndrome
Low-dose aspirin and low molecular weight heparin (LMWH) injections during pregnancy significantly improve outcomes.
Uterine abnormalities
Hysteroscopic surgery to remove septa, polyps, fibroids, or adhesions is highly effective.
Thyroid disorders
Thyroxine replacement for hypothyroidism reduces miscarriage risk significantly.
Chromosomal causes
Preimplantation Genetic Testing (PGT) with IVF allows selection of chromosomally normal embryos.

The importance of supportive care

Research consistently shows that couples with unexplained recurrent miscarriage who receive supportive care — regular early pregnancy scans, reassurance, and monitoring — have significantly better pregnancy outcomes than those who do not. Emotional support is an integral part of treatment.
