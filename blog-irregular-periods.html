Irregular Periods in Bangladesh — Causes, Diagnosis and Treatment

Irregular periods are one of the most common reasons women visit a gynaecologist in Dhaka. This guide explains the many possible causes, how they are investigated, and what treatments are available in Bangladesh.

Dr. Farzana Deeba September 2026 6 min read

What counts as an irregular period?

A normal menstrual cycle is between 21 and 35 days long. Periods that fall consistently outside this range — or that vary significantly from month to month — are considered irregular. A normal period lasts between 2 and 7 days. Bleeding that is much heavier, lighter, or shorter than usual can also signal an underlying issue.

Occasional irregularity is common and not always cause for concern — stress, illness, or travel can disrupt a cycle. It is when irregularity is persistent or accompanied by other symptoms that investigation is warranted.

Common causes of irregular periods in Bangladesh

Hormonal causes

  • PCOS (Polycystic Ovary Syndrome) — the most common cause of irregular periods in Bangladeshi women of reproductive age. Excess androgens disrupt ovulation, leading to infrequent or absent periods.
  • Thyroid disorders — both an underactive (hypothyroidism) and overactive (hyperthyroidism) thyroid can affect menstrual regularity.
  • Hyperprolactinaemia — elevated prolactin levels (the hormone responsible for milk production) can suppress ovulation and cause missed periods even without pregnancy.
  • Perimenopause — hormonal fluctuations in the years leading up to menopause can cause irregular cycles, often beginning in the early 40s.

Structural causes

  • Uterine fibroids — benign growths in or around the uterus that can cause heavy, prolonged, or irregular bleeding.
  • Endometrial polyps — small growths on the inner lining of the uterus that can cause irregular or spotty bleeding between periods.
  • Endometriosis — tissue similar to the uterine lining growing outside the uterus, often causing painful and irregular periods.

Lifestyle and other causes

  • Significant weight change — both excessive weight loss and obesity can disrupt hormonal balance and affect the menstrual cycle.
  • Stress — physical or emotional stress can temporarily suppress ovulation.
  • Excessive exercise
  • Certain medications — including antipsychotics, chemotherapy drugs, and some blood pressure medications.

When to see a specialist: If your periods are consistently shorter than 21 days, longer than 35 days, absent for 3 months or more, or accompanied by severe pain or very heavy bleeding, see a gynaecologist in Dhaka for evaluation.

How are irregular periods investigated?

A thorough evaluation typically includes:

  • Hormonal blood tests — FSH, LH, prolactin, thyroid function, testosterone, AMH
  • Pelvic ultrasound — to assess the uterus, ovaries, and endometrial lining
  • Medical history — cycle patterns, symptoms, medications, weight changes
  • Endometrial biopsy — in some cases, a small sample of the uterine lining may be taken for analysis

Treatment options for irregular periods in Dhaka

Hormonal treatment
Oral contraceptive pills, progesterone therapy, or hormonal IUD to regulate cycles and manage symptoms.
PCOS management
Metformin, lifestyle changes, and ovulation induction for women trying to conceive.
Thyroid treatment
Thyroxine replacement for hypothyroidism; antithyroid medication or radioiodine for hyperthyroidism.
Surgical treatment
Hysteroscopic removal of polyps or fibroids; laparoscopic treatment of endometriosis.
