When Should You See an Infertility Specialist in Dhaka?

Many couples in Bangladesh wait too long before seeking specialist help. This guide explains exactly when to seek a consultation, what tests to expect, and how to prepare for your first visit to an infertility specialist in Dhaka.

Dr. Farzana Deeba June 2026 5 min read

The standard guideline — and why timing matters

Medical guidelines recommend seeing a fertility specialist if you have been trying to conceive for 12 months without success (or 6 months if you are 35 or older). This is because fertility declines with age, and earlier intervention leads to better outcomes.

However, there are situations where you should not wait at all — and seeking help sooner can make a significant difference.

When to see a specialist immediately — without waiting 12 months

See a specialist now if you have any of the following:

  • Irregular or absent periods (fewer than 8 cycles per year)
  • A previous diagnosis of PCOS, endometriosis, or uterine fibroids
  • A history of pelvic inflammatory disease (PID) or STIs
  • Previous ectopic pregnancy or miscarriage
  • Known male factor — previous semen analysis showing low sperm count or motility
  • You have had cancer treatment (chemotherapy or radiation)
  • Your partner has had a vasectomy or testicular surgery
  • You are over 40 years old

Signs that something may need attention

Even if you are still within the 12-month window, certain signs suggest it is worth getting an early evaluation:

  • Painful periods — especially if the pain is severe enough to disrupt daily life, which can indicate endometriosis
  • Painful intercourse
  • Unusual discharge or pelvic pain
  • A previous pregnancy that ended in miscarriage — especially recurrent miscarriage (two or more)
  • Significant changes in period heaviness — much heavier or much lighter than before

A common misconception in Bangladesh: Many couples assume infertility is always a "female problem." In reality, male factor infertility accounts for approximately 40% of cases. A semen analysis for the male partner is one of the first and most important tests, and it is widely available in Dhaka.

What happens at your first consultation in Dhaka?

Knowing what to expect can ease anxiety before your first visit. At a specialist consultation in Dhaka, you can typically expect:

Medical history review

The specialist will ask about your menstrual history, previous pregnancies, any medical conditions or surgeries, and your partner's health history. Be as open and thorough as possible — everything you share is confidential and helps guide the evaluation.

Initial investigations

Depending on your history, the following tests are commonly arranged:

  • For the female partner: Blood tests (hormonal panel including AMH, FSH, LH, prolactin, thyroid), pelvic ultrasound to assess the uterus and ovaries
  • For the male partner: Semen analysis
  • If indicated: HSG (hysterosalpingography) to check whether the fallopian tubes are open

A personalised plan

Once results are reviewed, your specialist will discuss findings with you and recommend a treatment pathway — this may range from lifestyle changes and medication to IUI or IVF, depending on the cause identified.

How to prepare for your first visit

  • Note down the dates of your last 3–4 periods
  • Bring any previous test results, ultrasound reports, or medical records
  • If possible, attend with your partner
  • Write down any questions you want to ask — no question is too small
